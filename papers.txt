The largest single-solvent equilibrium set is CDCl3, n=9.
   7 of those share ONE donor
   (2-methylindoline, the Hemmer acceptor series), so they probe the ACCEPTOR
   axis only. The donor axis has 2 points (M1 dialkyl, M14 aniline).

Count of compounds is NOT the effective sample size. A 9-point series that
varies one substituent supports roughly ONE fitted parameter, not nine.
Leave-one-out CV within such a series measures interpolation, not
generalisation; grouped CV (hold out a whole paper/series) is the honest test.
